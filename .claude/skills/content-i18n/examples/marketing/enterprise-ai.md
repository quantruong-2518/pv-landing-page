# Enterprise AI

## Hero

**BAD**
> Empower your organization with seamless AI-driven transformation.

Fails: no subject doing anything, three loaded words carrying no information (`empower`, `seamless`,
`transformation`), and nothing a reader could check or disagree with. It would read identically for
any vendor in the category.

**GOOD**
> Connect internal company data to AI without moving it outside your infrastructure.

Works: names the mechanism (`without moving it outside`), the object (`internal company data`), and
the constraint that makes it worth buying. An evaluator can ask a follow-up question about it.

## Security section

**BAD**
> Military-grade security. Zero data leakage. Complete peace of mind.

Fails: `military-grade` is a marketing term with no standard behind it, `zero data leakage` is an
absolute guarantee no vendor can make, and the third line is a feeling. Two hard failures in nine
words.

**GOOD**
> Inference runs on hardware you control. When the model is deployed on-premise, requests do not
> leave your network.

Works: states the mechanism, then states the consequence **with its condition intact**. The
condition is the difference between a true statement and a lie.

## Deployment

**BAD**
> Flexible deployment options to suit your needs.

**GOOD**
> Deploy on-premise, in a private cloud, or in a hybrid setup.

Works: the reader now knows whether their environment is covered. "Flexible" made them ask.
