# B2B SaaS

## Pricing page lead

**BAD**
> Plans that grow with you. Simple. Transparent. Fair.

Fails: formulaic triple, three unverifiable adjectives, and a promise about the pricing model
without stating the pricing model.

**GOOD**
> Priced per active user per month. No minimum seat count, no annual commitment.

Works: two constraints a buyer can take to procurement.

## Feature section

**BAD**
> Powerful automation that streamlines your entire workflow, so your team can focus on what matters.

Fails: `powerful`, `streamlines`, `entire`, `what matters` — four abstractions, zero mechanism. "What
matters" is the tell: the writer did not know the customer's job.

**GOOD**
> New leads are classified, assigned an owner, and given a follow-up task — without anyone opening
> the CRM.

Works: three named steps and the thing that stops being necessary.

## Integration section

**BAD**
> Integrates seamlessly with the tools you already use.

**GOOD**
> Two-way sync with Salesforce and HubSpot. Everything else through the REST API or a webhook.

Works: names what is native, and is honest about what is not. The honesty is what makes the first
half believable.
