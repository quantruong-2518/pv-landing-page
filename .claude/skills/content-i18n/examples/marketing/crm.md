# CRM

## Homepage

**BAD**
> The all-in-one CRM that helps sales teams close more deals, faster.

Fails: `all-in-one` is a category claim every competitor makes; `close more deals, faster` is an
outcome claim with no evidence and no mechanism.

**GOOD**
> Every call, email and form submission lands on the lead record automatically. Your reps stop
> typing history and start reading it.

Works: names what the software does and the behaviour that changes as a result. The benefit is
implied by the mechanism, which is stronger than asserting it.

## Objection section

Most CRM pages skip this and lose the deal in silence.

**GOOD**
> **"We already have a CRM nobody uses."** Adoption fails when reps have to enter data twice. This
> one writes the activity record itself; the rep edits, not types.

Works: names the real objection in the buyer's words, then answers it with the mechanism.

## Migration

**BAD**
> Effortless migration from your existing system.

**GOOD**
> Import from a CSV or from Salesforce. Field mapping is manual — plan half a day for a
> 50,000-record account.

Works: the cost is stated. A buyer who plans for half a day and takes three hours trusts the next
claim on the page.
