# English

```
native · concise · factual · confident without hype · B2B professional
```

English is canonical in this repository, which makes it the locale most likely to be over-written.
Canonical means *first-class*, not *decorated*.

## Default sentence shape

```
subject + action + outcome
```

```
BAD   Empower your organization with seamless AI-driven transformation.
GOOD  Connect internal company data to AI without moving it outside your infrastructure.
```

## Words that need to earn their place

```
unlock · empower · seamless · revolutionize · redefine · game-changing
next-generation · cutting-edge · transformative · robust · holistic
future-proof · supercharge · elevate · unleash · powerful · innovative · state-of-the-art
```

Not banned. Each must carry meaning a plainer word would lose. "Robust" is fine in "robust to sensor
dropout" and empty in "a robust platform".

## Avoid

- **Adjective stacking** — *powerful, flexible, secure, intelligent*
- **Thought-leadership voice** — sentences about the industry rather than the product
- **Formulaic triples** — *Faster. Smarter. Better.*
- **Em-dash tic** — one per paragraph at most
- **Empty transformation claims** — *transform how you work*
- **Startup clichés** — *built different*, *we're on a mission to*
- **Hedged nothing** — *helps organizations to potentially improve* says less than silence

## Register

- Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels. Title Case only for proper nouns.
- Second person for the reader; first person plural sparingly for the company.
- Contractions are fine in product UI, optional in marketing, absent in legal lines.
- No exclamation marks. No rhetorical questions used as section openers.

## Mechanics

- Decimal **point**: `17.6 TOPS/W`. Thousands **comma**: `15,000`.
- Dates: `Sep 2026`, `May 2023`. Ranges with an en dash: `0.1–0.15 W`.
- Units keep a space: `10 W`, `5 × 5 mm²`. Multiplication factors do not: `~50×`.
- Serial comma in lists of three or more.
- No terminal period on buttons, labels or table headers; periods on full sentences.

## Product UI examples

| Context | Copy |
|---|---|
| Button | `Save changes` · `Delete campaign` · `Assign lead` |
| Error, known cause | `This lead is already assigned. Check the current owner before assigning it again.` |
| Error, unknown cause | `Could not load leads. Your filters are unchanged — try again.` |
| Empty, filtered | `No leads match these filters` |
| Success | `Campaign deleted. [Undo]` |

## Marketing example

```
BAD   Our next-generation, state-of-the-art AI platform empowers enterprises to unlock
      unprecedented efficiency and drive transformative growth.
GOOD  MINT runs inference inside memory at 17.6 TOPS/W and has been in mass production
      since May 2023. It fits a field device's power budget, so detection happens where
      the event happens.
```
